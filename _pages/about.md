Welcome to my personal website! I am a researcher at the **Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV)** and the **Instituto de Tecnología Química (ITQ-CSIC)** in Spain, where I am a Santiago's Grisolía Fellow.

My research lies at the intersection of materials science, green chemistry, and renewable energy. I am particularly interested in designing and characterizing advanced catalytic materials to address pressing environmental and energy challenges.

## Recent Work
My most recent projects focus on the application of **Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs)** and other nanostructured materials for sustainable energy applications. This includes:
- **Green Hydrogen Generation:** Exploring photocatalytic water splitting using doped MOF structures (e.g., Ti-Squarate MOFs) to achieve high-efficiency hydrogen production under visible light.
- **CO₂ Utilization:** Developing innovative catalytic and photo-thermal processes for CO₂ reduction to value-added chemicals like methanol and methane.
- **Cluster Strain Engineering:** Inducing structural reactivity in titanium frameworks to significantly enhance catalytic performance.

## Other Research Topics
Beyond photocatalysis and MOFs, my background encompasses various environmental technologies, including:
- **Biomass & Bio-oil Valorization:** Synthesizing hierarchically porous carbons and magnetic acid catalysts from bio-oil for wastewater treatment (removing emerging contaminants) and esterification reactions.
- **Desulfurization Technologies:** Scaling up green technologies for biogas desulfurization and sulfide oxidation, including the development of nitrogen-doped carbonaceous materials and the "Zaoki" catalyst, taking academic research from the bench to the market.
